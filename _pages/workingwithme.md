## Prospective Graduate Students

If you are interested in working with me as a graduate
student, please first apply to and be admitted to the
[Biomedical Informatics Training Program](https://www.dbmi.pitt.edu/education/applications/)
at the University of Pittsburgh.

Please do not contact me by email regarding research
opportunities or supervision before you have been
admitted to the program. Graduate admissions are
handled centrally through the program.

Once admitted, you are welcome to contact me to
discuss potential research opportunities.
